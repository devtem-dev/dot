Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Dev Open-Test (DevTemple DOT) Community ("we", "us") collects through testers.devtem.org, why we collect it and how we handle it.

1. What we collect

We only collect what you give us when you fill in the "Request a tester" form:

  • Your name and email address
  • The website address you want tested
  • The type of testing you need
  • Any details you choose to add
  • Your confirmation that you own the site or have permission to test it

We do not ask for passwords, payment details or access credentials through the form. Please do not send them.

2. What we do not collect

This site does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or analytics scripts, and it does not create user accounts.

3. How we use your information

  • To read your request and reply to you
  • To match you with a suitable verified tester and agree the testing scope
  • To prevent spam and abuse of the form
  • To keep a record that you confirmed you are authorised to test the site

4. Who handles your data

We use these providers to run the site. They process data on our behalf:

  • Cloudflare: receives your form submission through a Cloudflare Worker and may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver and protect the service.
  • Brevo: delivers your form submission to us as an email.
  • Netlify: hosts the website and may log standard request data such as IP address and browser type.
  • GitHub: tester profile pictures load from GitHub, so GitHub can see your IP address when the page loads.

When you share your request details with a verified tester so they can quote or carry out the work, we share only what they need for that request. Links to WhatsApp and GitHub take you to services with their own privacy policies.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. How long we keep it

We keep request emails for as long as needed to handle your request and for up to [12 months] afterwards, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To use any of these rights, email us at the address below.

7. Security

The site is served over HTTPS and uses security headers. Form submissions are validated before they are emailed. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

This site is not meant for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
